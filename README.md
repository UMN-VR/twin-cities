# Twin Cities campus tour scene

The University of Minnesota Twin Cities campus tour as a
[FOSS Earth](https://github.com/foss-earth/app) scene: 60 panoramas in 23 stops,
from Northrop Mall to the Saint Paul campus. [scene.json](scene.json) is the
scene document the [tour](https://github.com/UMN-VR/tour) shows at
https://umn-vr.github.io/tour/twin-cities/.

The photographs the scene points to are published with the site under
`/tour/twin-cities/media/`, not kept here. The scene and its photographs are
built together from a backup of the YouVisit tour by the tour's
`tools/twin-cities/build-scene.mjs` (see its README).

The [Minneapolis](https://github.com/UMN-VR/minneapolis) and
[Saint Paul](https://github.com/UMN-VR/st-paul) tours are each made from this
scene's panoramas on that campus.
